# AWANA Blue Jewels: Club Tools and Record-Keeping (as of 2026)

Scope note: Searches were run in November 2026. No official AWANA Club Manager help page on Blue Jewels was found. Most useful material came from third-party club-software docs (Approved Workman / AWdb) and individual church handbooks. Treat church-level claims as local practice, not AWANA policy.

## Does AWANA's official club software track Blue Jewels?

### Takeaway
No official AWANA-published software documentation on Blue Jewels was found. The best-documented tracking tool is Approved Workman (AWdb), a third-party database. It tracks handbook sections and book awards per member per club year, but its docs do not mention jewels by name.

### Cited Findings
- Approved Workman "Club module" article: the TODAY view shows per-member achievement for the current club date, including required and extra-credit section counts and points. The YEAR view has a Books page that "lists any book in which a member has completed a section (in the current club year)," with percent complete for required sections and the award earned on finishing. The article also has an Awards page listing awards earned per member. Last modified 27 Oct 2026. Source: [Approved Workman Club module](https://approvedworkman.freshdesk.com/solution/articles/16000021427-club-module)
- The same article says the term "jewels" is not used, so jewel tracking is not confirmed in that tool. Source: [Approved Workman Club module](https://approvedworkman.freshdesk.com/solution/articles/16000021427-club-module)
- Approved Workman's database lets leaders assign handbooks, including historical ones that can be marked completed on a past date. Source: [Approved Workman cloud database](https://approvedworkman.com/cloud-database) (search snippet only; page not fetched)
- Sparks jewel structure, from a 2026-2027 church parent letter (search snippet only; PDF text could not be extracted): 12 achievement jewels, 4 each of red (Bible drill sections), green (activity sections), and blue (club attendance). Clubbers must pass all sections in each jewel section. Source: [Sparks Letter to parents 2026](https://irp.cdn-website.com/117e96db/files/uploaded/Sparks+Letter+to+parents-2026.pdf); a Sparks parent handbook also describes the same colors. Source: [Parent Handbook](https://storage2.snappages.site/9PGTJH/assets/files/Parent-Handbook.pdf)
- Planning Center Check-Ins: described as a child and volunteer check-in and attendance system. Nothing found links it to Awana handbooks or jewels. Source: [Planning Center Check-Ins](https://planningcenter.com/checkins) (search snippet only)
- Awana's own blog suggests Mozo, an AWANA record-keeping tool. Source: [Awana blog, "whos counting"](https://www.awana.org/2016/05/24/whos-counting) (search snippet only; the 2016 date predates the 2026 scope, so treat as dated)

### Inferences
- Blue Jewels in the Sparks structure are tied to club attendance, so an attendance record is the likely source of blue-jewel credit. This is inferred from the parent-letter wording, not from software docs.
- Approved Workman's per-member, per-club-year Books view suggests that unfinished books would still show in the database for the club year. This is an inference about the tool's design, not a confirmed behavior.

### Gaps
- No official AWANA Club Manager help page on jewels was found.
- No Planning Center + Awana integration was found. The DerbyNet search returned nothing relevant, and DerbyNet is a pinewood-derby race tool, not a progress tracker.
- Mozo's feature set and whether it tracks jewels were not verified.

## Do published resources describe carrying a partly completed book into the next club year?

### Takeaway
One church handbook gives a simple rule: a clubber who finished last year's book gets the next one, and a clubber who did not finish continues where they left off. No official AWANA document was found that states this rule.

### Cited Findings
- A club guide (search snippet) states: if the child is in the same club as last year and has completed their book, they receive the next book; if not, they continue where they left off. Source: [Awana Handbook for club year 2025-2026](https://storage2.snappages.site/N2DGD8/assets/files/Awana-Handbook-for-club-year-2025-2026-76.pdf) (snippet only; the excerpt came from search, not a full fetch)
- Approved Workman's new-club-year setup article says that on promotion to a new base club, the uniform and current-handbook fields are cleared. It does not say whether book progress or jewel history is cleared. Modified 10 Aug 2026. Source: [Approved Workman, setup a new club year](https://approvedworkman.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/16000213066-setup-a-new-club-year)
- Awana's April 2024 blog post, "this one thing will make next club year easier," is a year-end checklist. It asks leaders to base fall handbook orders on book finishers and kids' ages. It does not describe carry-over rules. Source: [Awana blog, April 2, 2024](https://www.awana.org/2024/04/02/this-one-thing-will-make-next-club-year-easier/)
- Sparks pacing: one source notes Sparks needs to average about 3 sections per evening to finish a book within one year. Source: [Parent Handbook](https://storage2.snappages.site/9PGTJH/assets/files/Parent-Handbook.pdf) (search snippet)
- T&T clubbers in some clubs have an extra "Timothy award" book, done at the child's own pace, separate from the three-year rotation. Source: search snippet from a T&T club handbook (URL not confirmed)

### Inferences
- The "continue where they left off" rule appears to be a common local convention. Because it is a single church's guide, it should not be treated as AWANA-wide policy.

### Gaps
- No official AWANA Awana Basics or Club Manager page on carry-over was found. The Awana Basics "Record Keeping for Each Age Group" module (https://awanabasics.awana.org/secretary/record-keeping-for-each-age-group) exists, but its session content requires a login. The only public text is the module outline, with sessions for Puggles, Cubbies, Sparks, T&T, and Trek/Journey.
- The Bridge-Ahead Guide (https://www.awana.org/bridgeaheadguide/) was found in search but not fetched, so its record-handling guidance is unverified.

## What do club secretaries or administrators report doing for record-keeping across years?

### Takeaway
No club-secretary account of cross-year Blue Jewel handling was found in public sources. The only public signal is the club-year setup behavior in Approved Workman, which resets many fields while leaving the database record in place.

### Cited Findings
- Approved Workman: on a new club year, the setup defaults to the previous year's clubs and assignments. Completing the last grade sets a member to Graduated. Active members are set to inactive until registrations are processed. Members not selected for archiving remain in the database. Source: [Approved Workman, setup a new club year](https://approvedworkman.freshdesk.com/support/solutions/articles/16000213066-setup-a-new-club-year)
- Approved Workman's Points setup is per club year, and mid-year point changes recalculate all members, including past sessions in that year. Source: [Approved Workman Club module](https://approvedworkman.freshdesk.com/solution/articles/16000021427-club-module)
- Printed achievement cards: the Cubbies achievement card records weekly progress, attendance, handbook progress, and awards on one two-sided card, and the Sparks and Journey versions are sold through the Awana store. Source: [Awana Store, Cubbies achievement card](https://store.awana.org/product/cubbies-achievement-card-download/) (search snippet)

### Inferences
- Clubs using printed cards likely keep progress on the card, which travels with the child; clubs using AWdb keep it in the database per club year. Both are inferences from product descriptions, not from interviews with secretaries.

### Gaps
- No forum posts or blog posts from club secretaries describing their Blue Jewel practice were found in search.
- The Approved Workman club-year article does not say whether achievement history is retained or reset after a club-year rollover. Confirm with Approved Workman support.

## Source limitations
- Several key PDFs (the 2026 Sparks parent letter, an Awana secretary guide) could not be extracted by the fetch tool. Those claims come from search snippets and are marked as such.
- Dates: Approved Workman pages were modified in Aug and Oct 2026. The Awana blog post is dated 2 Apr 2024. The Awana Mozo blog post is dated 2016.
