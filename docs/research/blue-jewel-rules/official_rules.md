# AWANA Sparks Blue Jewel Rules (as of 2026)

## Bottom line
- No official AWANA (awana.org / store / club-resource) document I could read states the Blue Jewel rule. Every readable attendance rule came from individual church Sparks materials.
- The premise needs correcting: the sources I found describe Blue Jewels as **attendance awards earned per club quarter**, not as jewels earned by completing a book. Book-completion jewels (Red/Green in the church materials) are separate, and the pilot wing holds 12 of them.
- Several PDFs (AWANA awards packet, Sparks curriculum overview, church parent letters and handbooks) returned unreadable binary content through WebFetch, so their exact wording is unverified. Their content below comes only from search-result snippets.

## Key question 1: Per book or per club year? How many, at what thresholds?

### Takeaway
Church materials say Blue Jewels are attendance-based and awarded once per club quarter (four per club year). The attendance threshold varies by church (commonly two or fewer misses per quarter). Not per book.

### Cited Findings
- Blue attendance jewels are awarded for each of the four quarters of the AWANA year; one church's guide lists four blue jewels per year alongside other awards — [Sparks Parent Letter 24-25 (church)](https://www.springcreek.church/images/uploads/Awana%2024-25/Sparks%20Parent%20Letter%2024-25%20(website).pdf) (search snippet; PDF not readable); see also [Sparks Parent Letter 23-24](https://www.springcreek.church/images/uploads/Sparks_Parent_Letter_23-24.pdf) (search snippet)
- A clubber who misses two or fewer nights in a quarter receives that quarter's blue jewel — [church Sparks letter](https://www.springcreek.church/images/uploads/Sparks_Parent_Letter_23-24.pdf) (search snippet). A different church says the cutoff is "not miss more than 2 weeks out of the quarter" and another says blue jewels are "earned once a quarter based on club attendance (usually attending 5-6 times/quarter)" — [Sparks Parent Letter 24-25](https://www.springcreek.church/images/uploads/Awana%2024-25/Sparks%20Parent%20Letter%2024-25%20(website).pdf); [Sparks Info 2025-2026](https://storage2.snappages.site/F385K5/assets/files/2025-2026-Sparks-Info.pdf) (search snippets). Thresholds conflict across churches.
- Blue jewels are "determined by the Awana secretaries" based on attendance — [church guide, search snippet](https://www.springcreek.church/images/uploads/Awana%2024-25/Sparks%20Parent%20Letter%2024-25%20(website).pdf).
- Red and green jewels come from completing book sections; blue jewels come from attendance — same church guide, search snippet.
- Blue jewels are added to vests in January and April at one church — [Sparks Parent Letter 23-24](https://www.springcreek.church/images/uploads/Sparks_Parent_Letter_23-24.pdf) (search snippet). This is a distribution schedule, not a rule.
- Pilot Wing Badge store listing: "The design of this badge keeps 12 Achievement Jewels in place." Colors: HangGlider (Green), WingRunner (Blue), SkyStormer (Red) — [AWANA Store, Pilot Wing Badge](https://store.awana.org/product/pilot-wings-badge/) (verified by fetch). The page does not mention blue jewels or attendance.
- Church handbook snippet: kindergarten Sparks fill a green wing, first grade a blue, second grade a red, with four of each color available — [Parent Handbook (church)](https://storage2.snappages.site/9PGTJH/assets/files/Parent-Handbook.pdf) (search snippet; PDF not readable on fetch, so unverified).

### Inferences
- The "Blue Jewel = earned with the pilot wing for each book" model in the question is not supported by these sources. Blue jewels are attendance-based.
- The blue colour of the attendance jewel and the blue (WingRunner) pilot wing are different items. Do not conflate them.
- The official AWANA store confirms a wing holds 12 jewels, but I found no official store or handbook text tying the wing's jewels to book completion.

### Gaps
- No official awana.org page, Sparks Leader guide, or club-resource document (2026 or earlier) was readable that states the attendance threshold, the count, or the per-quarter rule.
- The official AWANA awards packet ([clubs.awana.org Awards packet, 2018](https://clubs.awana.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/04/Awards-packet.pdf)) and the [Sparks Curriculum Overview](https://awanabasics.awana.org/wp-content/uploads/protected/2021/07/SparksCurriculumOverview.pdf) could not be read; the awards packet is 2018, so it would be an older-edition source regardless.

## Key question 2: If a child finishes a book late in the club year and starts the next, does the next book's count start at the new book or at the club year?

### Takeaway
Not answerable from the official material I could access. Under the church-based attendance model, the count runs by club quarter, not by book, so book start/finish dates would not matter.

### Cited Findings
- None found. The only relevant statements are the quarter-based attendance rules above (church sources, search snippets).

### Inferences
- If the attendance model holds, a book-start date should have no effect on Blue Jewels, because jewels are tied to quarterly attendance.

### Gaps
- No source addresses book-finish timing for Blue Jewels. Official awards or handbook text would be needed.

## Source notes and reliability
- Search-result snippets from individual church sites (springcreek.church, snappages, etc.) are secondary and local. They conflict on thresholds (two misses, two weeks, 5-6 of 6 attendances) and are not official AWANA rules.
- Only the [AWANA Store pilot wing page](https://store.awana.org/product/pilot-wings-badge/) was a readable official-domain source.
- The AWANA awards packet (2018) is an older edition. It could not be read.
- Recommendation: confirm with the local Sparks director or the current Sparks Leader Handbook. Ask specifically whether Blue Jewels are attendance-based by quarter and what the threshold is.
