# An internet awards guide, checked against Awana's own pages (2026-11-14)

Mike supplied a "Complete Guide to Awana Curriculum Awards (Cubbies through Citation)" found on the Internet. It is not authoritative, so its main claims were checked against awana.org and the Awana store (the 2019 catalog, the 2021 Sparks progression chart, the 2018 curriculum guides and store product pages; some of these are dated, and the current leader guide should have the last word). The guide itself is not kept in the repository.

## The guide is wrong or unsupported

| Guide says | Awana's pages say | The app |
|---|---|---|
| Pilot Wings are earned by completing the Rank section | The wings are given with the first jewel; Rank earns the book's emblem (HangGlider green, WingRunner blue, SkyStormer red) | Matches Awana |
| Blue Jewels are earned for attendance or for bringing a guest | Attendance only | Matches Awana |
| Cubbies handbook has 24 Bear Hug lessons; no Trail Emblem | 26 Bear Hug lessons plus special-day sections; a Trail Emblem for the handbook's Trail section | Matches Awana |
| T&T: 8 emblems of 4 sections, 32 sections | A section award after every four sections, Start Zone sections counting toward the first | The app's 3/3/4/4/4/4/4/4 split is how the club counts Start Zone; do not change it to the guide's |
| Frequent Flyer pin; Essentials Certificate and Book Completion Pin | Not found on official pages | Not in the app |

## Confirmed

- Silver and Gold extra credit: one emblem per unit when all that unit's extra-credit sections are done, up to four of each a year.
- Sparks: Review Emblem after finishing a handbook and its review; Sparky Award plaque and pin for all three handbooks.
- T&T cumulative awards by number of books finished: Alpha 1, Excellence 2, Challenge 3, Timothy 4.

## Still unknown

- Which sections earn the T&T **Discovery Award emblems** (Blue, Red, Green, Yellow) and the **Extra Credit Silver and Gold emblems**. The store's "T&T Award Emblems" page (item MV-TTUltimateAdvDiscoveryEmblems) says only that they "can be earned for finishing handbook sections". The T&T director or the current T&T leader guide can settle it. This is what blocks the new T&T printed progress sheet (see `../../BACKLOG.md`).

Trek, Journey and the Citation Award are in the guide but outside the app's scope (Cubbies, Sparks, T&T).
