On top of that, nothing protects the night's entries if the phone browser drops the tab.
4. **Choose the quarter from the calendar, not from a hard-coded "Q2".** In a fresh browser on 2027-01-15, To do says "Needs a Q2 color team" and the Roster shows the Q2 column, while Attendance is already on 1/13 (Q3). (app.js:8, app-todo.js:11, app-season.js:103)

In my run that was the older one, with no warning (app.js:515-528). And with two leaders on two phones, whoever downloads last wins: nothing merges their files.

## Part 1: Calculation correctness

Date handling is solid. Every weekday and shift uses `T12:00:00Z`, `today()` is local (`en-CA`), and file tags use local time. I found no timezone bug. The CSV parser handles the BOM, CRLF, embedded quotes and newlines (test case 5 survives). `Season.totals`, `kidGamePoints`, `teamOn` (one-night team, then latest move in the quarter on or before the date, then the quarter default), `standings`, store maths and Statistics agree with each other on the test data: Statistics' "points" equals the store's raw total with the awards weight at 0 for all 106 children. Undo and History capture changes made on the store tab (verified in the browser).

### CONFIRMED (reproduced)

**B1. Bug: an empty night counts as a night held, so perfect-attendance stars disappear.** season.js:70, reports-season.js:109
- *Cause:* `setMark(..., 0)` runs `s.attendance[date] || (s.attendance[date] = {})`, so unticking on an unentered night creates `{}`. Deleting the last mark leaves `{}` as well (line 74 deletes only the child).
- *What follows:* the night picker shows ✓, and the Attendance Summary's `nightsSoFar` counts every attendance key in the calendar. Statistics and the store tab count only nights with at least one person present.
- *Repro (browser, `ui3.js`):* tick then untick the first child on 11/4. Result: `attendance["2026-11-04"] = {}`, picker label "Wed 11/4 (Q2) ✓". Attendance Summary: "every night so far (8)", and **0** children starred (28 before). Statistics still says "28 children have been here every night (7)". "Clear this room" on an unentered night does the same.
- *Fix:* don't create a night when clearing, and delete the night once it is empty. Add one helper, for example `Season.heldNights(s, cal)` = counted dates with at least one `a`, and use it in reports-season, store and stats.

**B2. Bug: children marked inactive by hand are reactivated by every import.** model.js:160, 196
- *Cause:* `refresh()` sets `x_Active = '1'` on every matched person, and `m.reactivate` makes `needsReview` true.
- *Repro (`e3.js` (a)):* mark one child inactive, then import the matching 10/28 export. Review is forced ("Reappearing (will be set active)"), and after Apply the child's `x_Active` is "1". There is no checkbox to keep them inactive.
- *Why it matters:* To do tells you to "Mark them inactive on the Roster" for Cancelled registrations and duplicates, both of which stay in the export.
- *Fix:* record who deactivated the person (for example `x_InactiveBy: import|user`). Only auto-reactivate people the import deactivated; for the others, show an unticked "make active again" box, and don't force a review every week.

**B3. Bug/risk: picking several data files opens the last one in the list, silently.** app.js:515-528
- *Repro (`ui3.js` (1)):* pick `…-20261030-090000.json` (has a 10/28 night) and `…-20261029-214815.json` together. The older file wins (no 10/28 night) and no dialog appears, because `openSeason` only asks when the session is already dirty.
- *Why it matters:* the splash invites multi-select ("hold Ctrl or Shift").
- *Fix:* when there are several season files, open the one with the newest file tag or latest history time, and say which.

**B4. Bug: the default quarter is hard-coded to Q2 and kept per browser.** app.js:8; app-todo.js:11 (`quarterOf(today)` falls back to `settings.quarter` on any day that is not a club night); app-season.js:103
- *Repro (`ui7.js`, clock set to 2027-01-15, fresh browser):* `settings.quarter = Q2`; To do shows "Needs a Q2 color team"; the Roster column is "Q2 team"; Attendance's default night is 2027-01-13 (Q3). The quarter-based report sheets would also print Q2. `DEFAULTS.dates` hard-codes this year's Q2 dates too.
- *Fix:* default to the quarter of the latest counted calendar night on or before today. Keep the manual choice only as an override that expires.

**B5. Bug: a child who changes group mid-season gets their old game points looked up in the new group.** store.js:55, stats.js:59, reports-season.js:114 all pass the child's *current* group to `kidGamePoints`.
- *Repro (`e6.js` 1):* a Sparks child with 10 place / 17 spirit points is moved to "T&T Boys (Group A)" with `setField`. The store then counts 8 place points, taken from the T&T games of the same color on the same nights.
- *Likelihood:* realistic early in the season, because To do's "Class does not match the grade" invites exactly this fix.
- *Fix:* stamp the group on the attendance mark when it is ticked (`attendance[d][id].g`), or keep a room-move log like `teamMoves`; fall back to the current group.

**B6. Bug (low frequency): Blue Jewels ignore the calendar.** season.js:88-98
- *Cause:* `blueJewels(s, id)` takes no calendar and counts every attendance date, while `totals` counts only club nights (not Event, not No Club).
- *How you reach it:* mark a night, then change that calendar row to Event or No Club, or delete it.
- *Repro (`e2.js`, attendance put on the 10/03 Event row to stand in for that):* the child has 5 nights, yet Blue Jewel 1 ("6 weeks") is reached on 10/21. The Awards tab would suggest it, and the Achievement Record would print it.
- *Fix:* pass `calRows` and skip dates that are not counted. Also add a Validate warning: "attendance/awards on N dates that are not club nights in the calendar". Today that data silently drops out of every total.

**B7. Bug: handbook text is misparsed when a word starts the string.** season.js:205
- *Cause:* `([A-Za-z]{2,5})?` takes any leading word.
- *Repro (`e4.js`):* "Returned" becomes code "Retur" + note "ned". "Ordered 260916" becomes code "Order", and the date is lost into the note. Typing a book name in the code box ("Hang Glider", the datalist shows names) saves as "Hang Glider 260916" and reads back as code "Hang" with **no date**. A note that starts with six digits, with no date filled in, turns into a date on reload.
- *Fix:* only take a code followed by a space, a digit or the end (`(?=\s|\d|$)`). Check the code box against known codes (2-5 letters, no spaces), or store `{code, date, note}` as an object.

**R1. Risk: a sibling swap is pre-ticked as a rename.** model.js:165-180, app.js:151-152
- *Cause:* within the same registration the similarity score compares full names, and the shared last name dominates it.
- *How often:* in the TEST export, 12 of 83 sibling pairs score at least 0.6, which pre-ticks the rename.
- *Repro (`e3b.js`):* a T&T girl with 7 nights leaves, and a Pre-K sibling with a two-letters-different first name joins. The rename is offered with score 0.83 and pre-ticked. After Apply, the toddler is in Cubbies carrying the older child's ID, nights and awards. The review row shows only the two names: no room, grade or birthdate.
- *Fix:* compare first names only when the registration matches. Show room, grade and birthdate in the rename table. Don't pre-tick when the birthdate differs. Match greedily by best score across all pairs, not in file order.

**R2. Risk: people added by hand are pre-ticked for deactivation on every import.** model.js:151, app.js:156-157
- *Repro (`e3.js` (c)):* `addManualPerson` ("Visiting Kid"), then the 10/28 import lists them under "In master but not in the new export", ticked.
- *Fix:* leave out `Registration ID = manual` (or show them unticked).

**R3. Risk: a returning child under a new registration ID splits into two people.** model.js:151 excludes inactive people from rename candidates.
- *Repro (`e3.js` (d)):* an inactive Sparks child reappears with a new registration ID, so the plan has 0 renames and 1 added. Their history stays on the inactive copy. The same child while still active is correctly paired ("same name, new registration ID").
- *Fix:* include inactive people in the same-name pairing, with the reason "returning child".

**R4. Risk/nit: name matching drops accented letters.** model.js:44
- *Cause:* `norm('Zoë') = "zo"` and `norm('Nuñez') = "nuez"`; `norm('José') === norm('Jos')`.
- *Result:* a Planning Center spelling fix from "Zoe" to "Zoë" breaks the exact match and falls through to the rename heuristic, and duplicates can be flagged falsely.
- *Fix:* `s.normalize('NFD').replace(/[̀-ͯ]/g, '')` before stripping.

**R5. Risk: Statistics and the other screens disagree on two rules.** stats.js:45, 54, 66
- *Inactive children:* "Most nights present" and the perfect count include them; points leaders, pace, the store and the Attendance Summary don't. Repro: the top attender marked inactive is still listed, and perfect stays at 28.
- *Excluded awards:* "Most awards" and Sparks pace count the awards in `config.exclude` (Flight 3:16, Start Zone 1/2, which everyone gets); the store does not. 45 of 84 children have different award counts on the two screens.
- *Fix:* one rule for each, or say it on screen.

**N1. Nit: class Share can go over 100%.** stats.js:41
- *Cause:* the average includes inactive children's past nights, while "enrolled" counts only active children.
- *Repro:* 8 children in Sparks A marked inactive gives a share of 135%.

**N2. Nit (UX): the store projection.**
- *Top and Range:* "projected" always equals "so far", because the factor cancels out (0 of 84 differ); this is correct maths. Hide or explain the projection for those methods, and only offer "Scale weights" under Direct.
- *Direct:* `nightsTotal` counts past nights that were never entered (10/28 here) as future nights, giving ×4.71. Late joiners are under-projected, since the pace divides by every night so far, including nights before they registered.
- *Children who never came:* 8 children with zero earnings get the minimum of 15. Confirm that is intended.
- *Min above max:* typing min 300 with max 200 silently raises max to 300.

**N3. Nits.**
- The phone CSS `#testBanner button{display:block}` (style.css:226) overrides `hidden`, so "Try an import" stays visible after use.
- The Puggles and Cubbies Attendance Summary prints Handbook, Part. and Extra columns they never use.
- The "Download data file" button sets `dirty = false` as soon as the download starts, even if the save dialog is cancelled.
- The task says 23 reports; the dropdown has 18.

### SUSPECTED (read, not reproduced)

- **A pending change can outlive a Planning Center change, including across years.** If Planning Center moves to a third value, `ov:` keeps overriding it. The review does mark "(you have a pending change here)", but Apply keeps the override. `Season.newSeason` carries `ov:` (verified: `ov:Grade` survives), so last year's grade override would hide this year's grade.
- **Rollover carries last year's uniform order dates.** `newSeason` keeps `x_Uni_*` (verified). The "ordered" date then hides next year's "Uniform to order".
- **Report sheets mix quarters.** `teamAsOf` in reports.js:29 combines `settings.quarter` with a chosen date that may sit in another quarter.
- **Each tick re-reads the whole file.** Every edit does the full-file `syncData`, `diff` (twice) and `snapshot`: about 12 ms per tick in Node with 140 people (`e5.js`). A whole year of history stays small (0.87 MB, compare 42 ms), but low-end phones could feel it. Diffing only the touched section would fix it.
