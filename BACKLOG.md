## How to use it

- Every item has an ID and a checkbox. `[ ]` open, `[x]` done (add the date, version and test; then move the line to the Done log at the bottom).
- **Check** says how the item was last verified. `V 2026-11-14` means verified against the code that day; the test name is the thing that turns red when it is fixed.
- Open items that can be checked without a browser have a check in `dev/app/test/backlog-open-items.test.js`. That file lists them in `OPEN`; the run stays green while they are open and goes RED ("now passes: close it in BACKLOG.md") the day one is fixed. When you fix an item: remove it from `OPEN`, tick it here.
- Priority: **P0** before the next club night; **P1** a leader can be hurt by it (privacy, wrong paper); **P2** wrong or missing content on a printout or screen; **P3** polish and cleanup; **W** waiting on a date or an answer.

**What is decided**
- **Who:** volunteers only. Children's absences (from parents) are not recorded here.
- **What is recorded:** club nights only, not date ranges. Each club night the volunteer is out is its own entry. The rest of the week does not matter.
- **Entry:** pick a volunteer, a From date and a To date, and an optional reason. Club nights in that range (from the calendar) are recorded as one absence, with each night as its own line.
- **Clearing:** clearing removes records and is always one of three choices: this night only, the whole absence, or the remaining nights of the absence. Each removed record is deleted; History keeps it for Undo.
- **Club nights, not weeks:** nothing is defined as a calendar week. "Upcoming" means the club nights from the club calendar, whatever weekday the club meets (Wednesday, Sunday, Saturday, or another day). The club calendar is used for this, not a fixed day.
- **Listings:** there is no separate Absences tab. Volunteer absences live in the existing top-level **Attendance** tab. When the Volunteers group is chosen there, the Attendance tab shows volunteer absences instead of child attendance (the logic is inverted for volunteers: the tab shows absences, not attendance). Two views: **All** (every absence, past and future) and **Upcoming** (only club nights on or after today). The view choice is kept in the tab's ui state, like other tab view choices. Update the "How it works" (Help) entry for Attendance to describe this. Layout of the volunteer view: one row per absence, grouped by club night.
- **Data shape** (follows the flags pattern): `absences[absId] = { id: personId, reason, at, nights: { 'YYYY-MM-DD': 1 } }`. The absence id is made like a flag id: the letter 'a' (flags use 'f') followed by the time in base 36 and two random base-36 characters, with no date in it. The 'a' prefix is documented in DATA-FORMAT.md with the new section, so anyone reading the file knows what it means. Nights are an object, not an array (`diffInto` treats an array as one value, so History could not record one night). Clearing a night deletes its key; clearing the last night deletes the absence (DATA-FORMAT §2.4: a record that becomes empty is deleted). "Remaining nights" means nights on or after today.
- **Non-club dates:** the nights in a From-To range come from `Calendar.nights(season.calendar)` (`isNight`: not an Event, not No Club). A range with no club night in it is refused with a plain message, and no date that is not a club night is ever stored.
- **A night later taken off the calendar** (deleted, or changed to No Club or Event): the absence keeps it and shows it marked "(no longer a club night)"; Validate lists it too. Past absences are never dropped silently.
- **Fewer than two club nights ahead:** "upcoming" counts today (date on or after today). The To do cards show what exists (one card if only one night is left); with no club night ahead on the calendar (or no calendar), one line says "No club nights ahead on the calendar."
- **Who can be picked:** active volunteers (`Model.isVolunteer` and `Model.isActive`). An absence whose person is later made inactive, leaves the roster, or is no longer a volunteer is kept and still listed, showing the name (or the id when the person is gone) with "(not on the volunteer list)".
- **To do count:** absences do not add to the To do number. They are information on the To do tab, not something to do.
- **No tick box:** entering an absence is the acknowledgement. Nothing else has to be ticked, so there is no Acknowledged check box and no acknowledged flag in the data.
- **Where it shows:** the top-level Attendance tab, with the Volunteers group chosen (see Listings). The Person page's Attendance sub-tab for a volunteer shows that volunteer's absences (the same records, filtered to that person). The data is stored with the person; the Attendance tab's volunteer view and the Person page are both aggregate slices of that one data set. The old message "Volunteers have no attendance record here." is replaced. On the To do tab, the two cards are the next two club nights from the calendar (shown with their dates), each listing that night's absences.
- **Past absences:** kept as a record, never hidden automatically.
- **Data format:** the new section is left out of the file while it is empty, so no version bump is needed (DATA-FORMAT.md §4.2).
